# Product

## Positioning

**Speaking first · Coaching second**

Mark Gordon equips leaders and organizations to build trust, strengthen relationships, and create cultures where people thrive. The public site’s primary job is to help the right person book Mark to speak — at conferences, organizations, leadership teams, nonprofits, and faith communities.

Relationship education remains core IP (Relationship Matters, courses, coaching, Blind Spot Assessment) but supports the leadership message; it does not lead the homepage story.

## Users

**Primary:** Organization and faith-community leaders, event planners, and HR/L&D buyers evaluating a keynote speaker, workshop facilitator, or leadership coach.

**Secondary:** Individuals seeking courses, the book, or coaching — routed through Resources / Online Courses in navigation, not homepage audience forks.

Visitors expect a personal, credible expert — not a course marketplace or faceless funnel.

## Product Purpose

Mark Gordon’s public site promotes leadership speaking, workshops, coaching, and relationship education. Success on the homepage means a stranger can answer in 30 seconds: who Mark is, what he speaks about, who he’s for, why it matters, and what to do next (**Book Mark to Speak**).

## Brand Personality

**Warm · Direct · Trusted**

Plain-spoken coach who respects your time. Expert confidence without corporate stiffness. Faith-informed where relevant; never preachy or exclusionary on the surface.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / startup templates (cream backgrounds, gradient text, hero-metric blocks, buzzword copy).
- Cold corporate consulting sites (stock imagery, impersonal tone, identical feature-card rows).
- Trendy minimal / AI-slop patterns (tracked uppercase eyebrows on every section, numbered section markers, identical icon-card grids).
- Online-course-marketplace homepages ($29 course cards competing with keynote positioning).

The site should feel like Mark Gordon, not a category template.

## Product Principles

1. **Elevate, don’t rebrand.** Keep cobalt blue, Montserrat/Open Sans, and familiar band rhythm; improve hierarchy, spacing, and polish for a speaking-first story.
2. **One primary action.** “Book Mark to Speak” dominates the homepage; secondary CTAs are contextual and lower on the page.
3. **Practice what you preach.** Clear CTAs, honest copy, no hype or manipulation.
4. **Proof over claims.** Testimonials, speaking topics, and Mark’s track record earn trust — not abstract mission paragraphs.
5. **Expert warmth.** Professional enough for church boards and corporate event planners; approachable without narrowing to “marriage coach” on the homepage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable components, alt text on images, and reduced-motion alternatives. No formal WCAG certification target; avoid obvious contrast and usability failures.
