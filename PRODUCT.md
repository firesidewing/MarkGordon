# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two co-primary audiences visit markgordon.ca:

- **Individuals and couples** looking for practical relationship guidance: online courses, the Relationship Matters book, coaching, and the Blind Spot Assessment.
- **Organization and faith-community leaders** booking keynotes, workshops, and packaged programs for their groups.

Both arrive with a specific need (learn, book, buy, or inquire) and expect a site that feels personal and credible, not a faceless funnel.

## Product Purpose

Mark Gordon's public site promotes relationship education, coaching, and speaking. It exists to help visitors discover the right offering, trust Mark's expertise, and take a clear next step (book a call, enroll, buy the book, or contact).

Success looks like: visitors quickly find what they need, feel welcomed and understood, and convert without friction. The rebuild from WordPress to Astro should preserve brand recognition while improving craft (typography, spacing, motion, performance).

## Brand Personality

**Warm · Direct · Trusted**

Voice is encouraging and plain-spoken, like a coach who respects your time. Expert confidence without corporate stiffness. Faith-informed where relevant, but never preachy or exclusionary on the surface.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / startup templates (cream backgrounds, gradient text, hero-metric blocks, buzzword copy).
- Cold corporate consulting sites (stock imagery, impersonal tone, feature-grid monotony).
- Trendy minimal / AI-slop patterns (tracked uppercase eyebrows on every section, numbered section markers as default scaffolding, identical icon-card grids).

The site should feel like Mark Gordon, not a category template.

## Design Principles

1. **Elevate, don't rebrand.** Keep the established blue, Montserrat/Open Sans pairing, and familiar layout patterns; improve hierarchy, spacing, and polish.
2. **Practice what you preach.** The site models healthy communication: clear CTAs, honest copy, no hype or manipulation.
3. **Serve both audiences equally.** Individuals and org leaders should each find a path without the site feeling split or favoring one over the other.
4. **Show, don't tell.** Lead with video, testimonials, course previews, and real content over abstract marketing claims.
5. **Expert warmth.** Professional enough for church boards and corporate event planners; approachable enough for couples at home.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sensible defaults: semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable components, alt text on images, and reduced-motion alternatives for animations. No formal WCAG certification target, but avoid obvious contrast and usability failures.
