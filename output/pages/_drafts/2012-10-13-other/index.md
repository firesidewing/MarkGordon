I am licensed with the Province of British Columbia to perform weddings. My philosophy for wedding ceremonies is, ‘It’s your day, so it’s your way.’ Each ceremony I conduct is uniquely crafted by me to suit the wishes and personality of the bride and groom. I love weddings and my passion for people is evident, underlined by a strong desire to help families be relationally healthy. This starts with helping make the wedding experience fun and memorable. My approach is flexible, and combined with a relaxed and friendly style, I help to put everyone involved at ease, even nervous grooms!
