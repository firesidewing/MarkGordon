# Speaking / Keynote page surface brief

## Mode

**Persuade** — event organizer decides to book Mark to speak.

## Job and audience

Conference organizers, leadership teams, nonprofits, churches, and businesses evaluating a keynote speaker. They need five answers in under 20 seconds: what Mark speaks about, who it's for, why he's different, what audiences gain, and how to book.

## Outcome and proof

**Primary action:** Book Mark to Speak (HubSpot discovery).

**Secondary action:** Schedule a Conversation (`/contact/`).

**Proof on page:** Signature keynote cards with takeaways, audience types, excerpt testimonials, event organizer FAQ.

## Section sequence (9 bands)

1. **Hero** — headline, subhead, credibility strip, dual CTAs, speaking photo
2. **Why Mark** — practical transformation, outcome bullets
3. **Speaking Philosophy** — relationships beneath leadership challenges
4. **Signature Keynotes** — 4 centerpiece cards + CTA
5. **Additional Topics** — expandable list
6. **Workshops teaser** — deeper engagement + Explore Workshops
7. **Audiences Mark Serves** — 4 audience cards
8. **Testimonials** — excerpts + Read More + CTA
9. **Event Organizer FAQ** — 5 questions
10. **Booking close** — final dual CTAs

## Scope and boundaries

### In scope

- Full page rebuild per customer feedback report (Aug 2026)
- Leadership & culture speaker positioning (not motivational speaker)
- Mobile: short hero, keynotes within ~2 scrolls, 3–4 line paragraphs max
- Repeat "Book Mark to Speak" after hero, keynotes, testimonials, close

### Out of scope

- Leadership Accelerator (page does not exist)
- New photography (use existing assets; produce via asset-producer if gaps found)
- `/book-now/` flow changes

## Mobile

First screen: headline → one intro paragraph → two CTAs. Keynote topics visible within seconds. No long bio before topics.
