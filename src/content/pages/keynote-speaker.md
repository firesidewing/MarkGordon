## KEYNOTE SPEAKER

Whether speaking in the faith community or a business, recovery agency or a non-profit or a leadership team, my goal is to be engaging and to always leave people with inspiration and practical life applications.

**My heart is to bring people inspiring and contextual content through Keynote communication.**  
My topics are relevant and empowering concerning relationships or the subjects that hurt or enhance them. Speak in a variety of settings and topics I am committed to understanding the audience I am speaking with. I am at my core, relational and so would rather be connecting with people than sitting in a greenroom. As a result, I authentically develop connections with the people I speak for. ![](../../assets/content/keynote-speaker/tsg-9.jpg)
